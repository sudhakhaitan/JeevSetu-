JeevSetu v0.1 Build 3.9 — V2-5-22
Baseline: V2-5-21

Changes:
1. Hindi-first complaint + HPI retained.
2. After completing HPI for a complaint, JeevSetu asks whether there is another complaint.
3. If patient says no, the encounter ends immediately and generates the Hindi clinical note.
4. If yes, the next complaint gets its own HPI cycle; this repeats until the patient says no.
5. Common questions avoid unnecessary English medical terms (e.g. constipation -> पेट साफ़ होने में दिक्कत).
6. Final note contains all complaints and their corresponding HPI sections.
7. English translation remains deferred.
