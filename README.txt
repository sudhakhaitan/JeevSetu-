JeevSetu V2-5-84 — Section 12 Trace Repair
==============================================

Repair made from V2-5-83 uploaded build.

Root cause found:
Section 12 dynamically generated buttons had BOTH inline onclick="s12_..." handlers
AND a delegated click listener on #s12App. A single tap could therefore execute
the same Section 12 action twice, changing s12_step/sub-step state twice and
leading to the displayed technical-error/no-progress behavior.

Repair:
- Removed the 9 inline Section 12 s12_* onclick handlers.
- Kept one delegated click listener on #s12App as the single event path.
- Preserved Section 12 answer/sub-answer/text/multi/confirm/restart functions.
- Preserved Section 12 persistence into jeevsetu_master_encounter.
- Preserved Section 12 -> Section 13 transition.
- No Clinical Note or Master Clinical Note is generated by Section 12.
- Final Master Clinical Note remains gated by verified Section 21 completion.

Static JavaScript syntax check: PASSED.


V2-5-85 repair: Fixed Section 12 s12_askSub() missing s12App container reference, which caused the Section 12 Yes button to show a technical error when entering follow-up questions. No other Section 12 navigation or clinical-note-generation logic was changed.
