Pregnancy screening is contextual: age, sex and menstrual status are collected first. Pregnancy status is not asked for males or for females whose menstruation has not started or has completely stopped. Pregnancy-related red-flag screening is considered only when pregnancy is relevant and the complaint involves bleeding or abdominal pain.
