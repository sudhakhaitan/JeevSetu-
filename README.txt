JeevSetu Section 17 — Anxiety & Depression Screening

Design principles:
1. Screening is OPTIONAL and is opened only after explicit patient consent.
2. The questionnaire is designed for private self-completion.
3. A non-medico history taker should not view individual PHQ-9/GAD-7 answers.
4. PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are screening instruments, not diagnostic tools.
5. PHQ-9 item 9 creates an urgent clinician-only alert when positive.
6. The final result shows scores and severity ranges to the authorized clinical reviewer.
7. Patient refusal is recorded without further probing by the non-medico history taker.
