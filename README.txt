JEEVSETU AI — SECTION 8
SUBSTANCE USE SCREENING
Standalone Prototype v1

Scope:
- Section 8 screens substance use.
- Categories: tobacco, alcohol, cannabis, opioids, sedatives/sleeping medicines, stimulants, inhalants, other.
- Records current use / past use / unknown, frequency and duration at screening level.
- Detailed alcohol history is intentionally reserved for Section 9.
- Generates a clinician-facing structured note.
- Current use produces clinician-attention text for further clinical assessment.
- Patient interaction labels are in Hindi/English.

This is a standalone prototype for workflow testing and is not a diagnostic instrument.
