JeevSetu Section 16 — Stress, Emotional & Psychological History
Integrated Optional Private Anxiety & Depression Screening

- Anxiety/depression screening is part of Section 16, not a separate section.
- At 16.10 the patient is explicitly asked for consent.
- NO: screening stops and there is no further probing.
- YES: private PHQ-9 and GAD-7 self-completion opens.
- Individual sensitive answers are intended to remain private from the non-medico history taker.
- PHQ-9 item 9 creates a clinician-only urgent alert when positive.
- Scores are screening results, not diagnoses.
